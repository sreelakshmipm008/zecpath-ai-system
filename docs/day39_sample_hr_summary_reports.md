## Reporting Notes

1. Scores must come from the existing HR interview scoring engine.
2. Strengths and weaknesses must be derived from available scoring results.
3. Cultural fit indicators must be grounded in actual interview responses.
4. Inconsistencies must be based on the consistency analysis.
5. Automated summaries support recruiter review and do not replace human judgment.
6. The examples in this document are illustrative templates, not verified candidate assessments.
