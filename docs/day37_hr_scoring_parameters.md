# HR Interview Scoring Parameters

## 1. Answer Relevance

Answer Relevance measures how closely a candidate's response addresses the question asked by the interviewer.

A higher score indicates that the response is directly related to the question, addresses the required topic, and provides appropriate information.

A lower score indicates that the response is partially related, vague, or does not adequately address the question.

---

## 2. Communication Score

Communication Score measures the effectiveness and clarity of the candidate's communication during the interview.

It considers how clearly the candidate expresses ideas, whether responses are understandable, and how effectively information is communicated.

A higher score indicates clear and effective communication.

A lower score indicates unclear, difficult-to-follow, or poorly structured communication.

---

## 3. Confidence Score

Confidence Score measures the level of confidence demonstrated by the candidate while responding to interview questions.

It represents communication signals associated with confidence, such as response delivery, hesitation patterns, and overall assurance in the response.

A higher score indicates stronger confidence signals.

A lower score indicates weaker confidence signals or noticeable hesitation.

---

## 4. Consistency

Consistency measures how stable and coherent the candidate's responses and communication signals remain throughout the interview.

It considers whether the candidate maintains a reasonably consistent level of response quality and communication across different questions.

A higher score indicates stable and consistent performance.

A lower score indicates significant variation or inconsistency across responses.

---

## Scoring Parameters Summary

| Parameter | Measures |
|---|---|
| Answer Relevance | How closely the answer addresses the question |
| Communication Score | Clarity and effectiveness of communication |
| Confidence Score | Confidence demonstrated during responses |
| Consistency | Stability of performance throughout the interview |
